About Onam

Onam is the most celebrated festival of Kerala, filled with happiness, unity, culture, and tradition. It is celebrated during the Malayalam month of Chingam, usually in August or September. The festival represents prosperity, harvest, and the legendary return of King Mahabali.

🌸 Pookalam

Beautiful flower decorations called Pookalam are created in front of homes during Onam celebrations.

🍛 Onam Sadya

A traditional vegetarian feast served on banana leaves, featuring a variety of delicious Kerala dishes.

👑 Mahabali

Onam celebrates the annual visit of the legendary King Mahabali, remembered for his generosity and kindness.
