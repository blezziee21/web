FESTIVALS OF INDIA

India is a land of Festivals. People from different parts of the country celebrate various festivals with great joy and enthusiasm.Diwali, Holi and Onam are some of the major festivals celebrated in India.

1. Diwali

Diwali is known as the Festival of Lights. People decorate their homes with lamps, candles and colorful decorations.It symbolizes the victory of light over darkness.

2. Holi

Holi is the festival of colours.People celebrate Holi by playing with colourful powders and water.It is a Festival of happiness, friendship and togetherness

3. Onam

Onam is a major festival of Kerala. It is celebrated with flower decorations,traditional dances,games and the famous onam feast calledOnasadya
