🪔 Diwali - Festival of Lights

Celebrating light, happiness, goodness and togetherness

✨ About Diwali

Diwali is the major Festival of Lights celebrated across India. It symbolizes the spiritual victory of light over darkness and good over evil. Homes are decorated with diyas, candles and colorful rangoli, creating a beautiful and joyful atmosphere.

🎉 Diwali Celebrations

  1. Lighting diyas and candles
  2. Decorating homes with colorful rangoli
  3. Exchanging sweets and gifts
  4. Spending time with family and friends
