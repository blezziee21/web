A Land of Celebrations

India is a land of diverse cultures, traditions and festivals. People from different parts of the country celebrate various festivals with great joy and enthusiasm. Diwali, Holi and Onam are some of the major festivals celebrated across India.

Popular Indian Festivals

🪔 Diwali

Diwali is known as the Festival of Lights. People decorate their homes with lamps, candles and colorful decorations. It symbolizes the victory of light over darkness.

Discover Diwali

🌈 Holi

Holi is the Festival of Colors. People celebrate Holi by playing with colorful powders and water. It is a festival of happiness, friendship and togetherness.

Discover Holi

🌸 Onam

Onam is a major festival of Kerala. It is celebrated with flower decorations, traditional dances, games and the famous Onam feast called Onasadya.

Discover Onam
